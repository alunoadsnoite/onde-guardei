Catalog objects with a photo, a name or both, and record where they are stored. Nest locations, search names and location paths without accent-sensitive matching, and keep a history of location changes.

Includes drafts and photo-inclusive backups through the Android file picker. Restore replaces the catalog after confirmation. Missing photos must be fixed before exporting.

Works offline without an account, ads, tracking or a cloud service. Data stays on the device. Backups are not encrypted: store them appropriately for the privacy of your photos. Uninstalling removes private app data.

The interface is available in Brazilian Portuguese only.
